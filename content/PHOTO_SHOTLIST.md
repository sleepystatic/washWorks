# Wash Works — photography shot list (A-6)

You're handling photography. Here's a prioritized list to shoot against. The site
is built to look finished *without* photos (typography + navy + the icon motif),
so these drop in as upgrades — no rush, but the top of the list moves the needle
most.

Shoot **landscape (horizontal), 3:2 or 16:9**, as large as the camera allows. I'll
optimize and resize whatever you deliver (see "Handing them off" below) — don't
pre-shrink or crop tight; leave breathing room so I can crop for different slots.

## Tier 1 — shoot these first (highest conversion impact)

1. **A finished MCC panel, door open, wiring visible.** The single most persuasive
   image on the site. Clean, well-lit, straight on. A UL508A-listed panel that
   *looks* like quality work sells the whole Motor Control Centers line.
2. **A technician on a real service call, in a branded shirt.** Hands on equipment,
   caught working — not posed. Buyers trust people they can see.
3. **The Lodi shop / building exterior**, ideally with signage and a service
   vehicle in frame. Grounds the business as real and local.
4. **A service vehicle** — clean, branded, on its own. Doubles as an "about/contact"
   image and reinforces that you come to them.

## Tier 2 — strong supporting shots

5. **Conveyor work** — chain, rollers, correlator. A tight, in-focus detail of the
   mechanism reads as expertise to anyone in the trade.
6. **A tunnel mid-wash** — brushes engaged, water and foam. The one "action" shot;
   good hero candidate.
7. **The custom ladder rack** you build — it's a differentiator, so show it.
8. **A central vacuum system / producer install** — for the vacuum service page.
9. **Reclaim or RO equipment** — the water-treatment gear, for that service page.

## Tier 3 — nice to have

10. **Before / after** on a rebuild — even phone snaps work if that's all you have.
11. **The team** — a group shot for the About page once we write the real story (C-5).
12. **Detail textures** — controls, gauges, a clean equipment rack — usable as
    quiet section backgrounds.

## Practical notes

- **Horizontal light beats overhead fluorescent.** Shoot panels and equipment near a
  bay door if you can, or bring one work light to fill shadows.
- **Wipe down** the hero subjects — a smudge reads as neglect on a company selling
  reliability.
- **Get model/property permission** if a customer's site or staff appear, so we're
  clear to publish.
- **No stock.** The handoff's flyer used what looked like stock — real equipment
  from *your* jobs is the entire point, and buyers can tell the difference.

## Handing them off

Drop the originals (full-size, unedited) into `brand-source/photos/` and tell me
which shot is meant for which slot (hero, MCC, conveyor page, etc.). I'll run the
image pipeline — resize, compress, and export AVIF + WebP with a JPG fallback and
correct `srcset` — so each one loads fast on mobile without layout shift (T-8).
You never have to think about formats or sizes; just give me the good originals.
